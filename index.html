Fun Facts About Potatoes

  • The average American eats 124 pounds of potatoes yearly, while the average German eats twice as much!
  • The largest potato ever grown weighed 7 pounds and 1 ounce
  • Potato blossoms became popular in royal fashion when Marie Antoinette wore them in her hair while passing through the French countryside
  • Potato chips were created by a passive-aggressive chef named George Crum who received a complaint about the thickness of the potatoes in the dish. In response, Crum sliced the potatoes as thinly as possible and fried them in oil with salt in hopes the crunchy dish would anger the customer. Instead, he loved it!
