About Dogs

Dogs have been companions to humans for thousands of years and were actually the first species to be domesticated by humans. They remain the most popular pets to this day, and with hundreds of breeds with personalities specific to each one, it's hard not to find a dog that meets each household's needs.

A Few Interesting Facts

  • Dogs are able to detect tiny changes in a human's scent. Their spectacular sense of smell allows them to smell chemical changes such as stress.
  • A dog's noseprint is unique to them,just like how every human has a unique fingerprint.
  • Dogs can see some colors, but not all like humans. They can mostly see blue and yellow while red and green don't appear distinctly to them.
  • Dogs can also dream in their sleep, which is why they sometimes twitch, move their paws, or even run!
